## Introduction

Assemble any of the **<!-- SERVICES:COUNT -->** supported services in real-time with ease.

Simply:

1. Search for a notification service by name or schema (e.g., `discord`, `email`, `slack`)
1. Provide the required settings needed to access the service through the provided fields:
  - **Required fields** are marked with an asterisk (`*`) and must be filled before the URL is usable.
  - **Sensitive fields** (tokens, passwords, API keys) are masked in the display; click the eye icon to reveal what you typed.
1. Press the **Copy** button to place your assembled Apprise URL into your clipboard.  You can now paste it back into any configuration that leverages the Apprise architecture/framework.


For more detail on URL syntax see the [Universal URL Syntax](/getting-started/universal-syntax/) guide.
