<section class="mobile-preview">
  <h2>Send It Now or Save It for Later</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <strong>Send from anywhere.</strong> Reach the people, apps, and services
      that matter from your phone.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Share photos and files.</strong> Take a photo or choose a file,
      share it to Apprise Mobile, select your targets, and send.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Reuse your messages.</strong> Full Version lets you save common
      messages and their targets as templates, then send them again on demand.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Connect quickly.</strong> Scan a QR code from the Apprise API Web
      interface to add a server and its configuration.
    </li>
    <li>
      <strong>Edit when needed.</strong> Work with remote configurations in Text
      or YAML using formatting, error hints, undo, and local conversion.
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>
