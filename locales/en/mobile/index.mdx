      <span class="mobile-beta-label">Android Beta</span>
      <h2>Your Apprise API, Wherever You Are</h2>
      <p>
        Apprise Mobile connects directly to your self-hosted Apprise API. Send notifications,
        manage services, build Apprise URLs, and review delivery results from your Android device.
      </p>
      <p>
        Requires a running <a href="/api/deployment/">Apprise API server</a> — Apprise Mobile is a
        client for your server, not a notification service on its own.
      </p>

<section class="mobile-beta-intro">
  <h2>Help Test the App</h2>
  <p>
    Apprise Mobile is in a closed Google Play test before its public release. We
    need at least 12 testers to remain enrolled for 14 continuous days. Joining
    the beta and trying the app helps us reach that goal and improve the
    experience for everyone.
  </p>
  <p>
    <strong>Use the same Google account for all three steps.</strong>
  </p>
  <div class="mobile-beta-order">
    Complete each step in order. The Google Play listing is unavailable until
    you have joined the group and Google recognizes your account as a tester.
  </div>
</section>

<div class="mobile-beta-steps" role="list">
  <article role="listitem">
    <div>
      <h2>Join the Google Group</h2>
      <div class="mobile-step-copy">
        Joining the group adds your Google account to the approved tester list.
      </div>
      <a
        class="mobile-step-link"
        href="https://groups.google.com/g/apprise-testers/"
        target="_blank"
        rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >
        Join Apprise Testers
      </a>
    </div>
  </article>
  <article role="listitem">
    <div>
      <h2>Become a Tester</h2>
      <div class="mobile-step-copy">
        Open the opt-in page and select <strong>Become a tester</strong>.
      </div>
      <a
        class="mobile-step-link"
        href="https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.appriseit.mobile"
        target="_blank"
        rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >
        Open the Opt-In Page
      </a>
    </div>
  </article>
  <article role="listitem">
    <div>
      <h2>Install Apprise Mobile</h2>
      <div class="mobile-step-copy">
        After Google confirms that you are a tester, open the listing and
        install the app.
      </div>
      <a
        class="mobile-step-link mobile-step-link-primary"
        href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.appriseit.mobile"
        target="_blank"
        rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >
        Open Google Play
      </a>
    </div>
  </article>
</div>
