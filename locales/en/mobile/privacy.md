**Last updated: August 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy applies to Apprise Mobile, published as **Apprise** on
Google Play ("the app").

## Overview

The app is local-first. We do not operate an app backend, require an account,
or include advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. The app
connects directly to the Apprise API Servers you choose to configure.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store:

- Server addresses, configuration IDs, display details, and credentials.
- Notification URLs, tags, templates, messages, attachments, and send history.
- Cached server configuration, service metadata, health results, and task
  results.
- App preferences, diagnostic logs, and records of accepted legal documents.
- Temporary copies of files you select or photos you take for an attachment.

Credentials are protected with the Android Keystore. Other app data remains on
your device unless you choose to send, export, or back it up.

Credentials, configuration IDs, and notification URLs may authorize your
server to send through connected accounts. Protect your device and backups,
use strong and unique credentials with a hard-to-guess configuration ID, and
replace or revoke them if you suspect they were exposed.

## Data Transmission and Privacy

Apprise Mobile does not send your personal data, credentials, notification
content, attachments, server details, or app activity to us, advertisers,
analytics providers, or any third-party service selected by us.

Server details, credentials, notification URLs, templates, messages, and other
app settings are stored locally on your device. Remote configurations remain on
the Apprise API Servers you configure, and the app may cache copies locally.

When you use a notification or server-management feature, Apprise Mobile
communicates directly and only with the Apprise API Servers you explicitly
configure. A request may include credentials, a configuration ID, notification
content, targets, tags, or attachments. When you add an attachment by URL, the
app may make a one-time request directly to that URL solely to check whether it
is reachable.

Any subsequent delivery to downstream notification services is performed
solely by your configured server. We do not act as an intermediary, monitor,
or receive that traffic. Your server operator and the notification services it
contacts may process or retain the information under their own policies.
Review those policies and your server configuration before sending sensitive
content.

Use HTTPS when possible. With plain HTTP, data sent between the app and your
server is not protected by transport encryption.

## Information We Do Not Receive or Use

We do not receive app activity, notification content, server details,
credentials, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, or crash reports. We do not
sell, rent, or use your information for advertising or tracking.

## Google Play Services

Google Play Billing processes an optional premium purchase. Google handles
payment details; the app receives only the purchase information needed to
provide and restore premium access. Your use of Google Play is governed by
[Google's privacy and security practices](https://play.google.com/about/privacy-and-security.html).

## Backups and Diagnostic Exports

Backup and diagnostic export are optional and begin only when you request
them. A backup stores credentials, notification URLs, and template content in
an encrypted section. The passphrase is optional; without one, anyone with the
backup can restore its secrets. Server addresses, usernames, display details,
and other non-secret settings may remain readable. Files are saved to the
location you choose and are never sent to us.

## Retention and Deletion

App data remains on your device until you delete it, an app retention setting
removes it, or you uninstall the app. Files exported outside the app remain
until you delete them. Data sent through your server is controlled by its
operator and the destination services. Because the app has no account or
developer-operated backend, there is no account data for us to delete.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly receive
personal information from children or any other users.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. When a change requires renewed
acceptance, the app will ask you to review the current policy before continuing.
