This page walks through the everyday parts of Apprise Mobile. Every picture has numbered callouts, with what each one does listed beside it. The pictures follow the light and dark switch at the top of this site, so you see the app in the same look you are reading it in.

If you have not connected the app to your server yet, start with [Set Up Apprise Mobile](/mobile/getting-started/).

## The Dashboard

The dashboard lists every service your server can send to, grouped under each Config ID. The colored bar on the left tells the Config IDs apart, and the line under the address shows whether the server answered its last check.

Tap one or more tags to choose who gets your next message, or tap a service to pick just that one. Then tap **Send** to write the message.

To send to one service without writing a full message first, swipe it to the right and let go. Apprise sends to that service only.

## Switching a Config ID Off and On

Swipe a Config ID's header to the left and let go to switch it off. It turns grey, and its services are left out of sends, health checks, and refreshes. Swipe left again to switch it back on.

## Templates

A template is a saved message, with its title, body, and who it goes to, ready to send again with one swipe. Templates need the full version of the app.

- Tap **Create** on the Templates screen to write a new one, or turn a message you already sent into a template from its delivery report.
- Swipe a template to the right to send it.
- Tap a template to see or change it, hold the handle on the right to move it up or down, or tap the bin to remove it.

Good candidates are messages you send often: "Dinner Is Ready", "On My Way Home", or "Running Late".

## Light and Dark

The app follows your phone's light or dark setting. To pick one yourself, open **Settings > Appearance**. The same menu has **Language**, where you can choose a language for the app that differs from your phone's.
