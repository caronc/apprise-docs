## 💙 Support Apprise

### For Service Providers and Companies

A sponsorship highlights your service where people go looking for it: a heart badge and priority placement in the [URL Builder](../../url-builder/) and [service listings](../../services/), and a supporter note on your service's own documentation page. Higher tiers add your name and logo to this page and put them in a rotating sponsor banner across all <!-- SERVICES:COUNT --> service documentation pages. That tells your users you invest in the open source tools they depend on, and it helps keep your integration maintained and up to date.

[**See how sponsorship works**](../how-to-sponsor/)

### For Individuals

If Apprise has saved you time or solved a problem, even a small contribution helps cover API costs and keeps the project active.
