## 💙 Support Apprise

### For Service Providers and Companies

If Apprise supports your notification service, or your company uses Apprise in production, consider a sponsorship. Last month Apprise received over 449,300 requests from 43,200 unique visitors — many of them configuring your service.

A sponsorship puts your name and logo in the [URL Builder](../../url-builder/) and [service listings](../../services/), and adds a rotating sponsor banner across all <!-- SERVICES:COUNT --> service documentation pages — signaling to your users that you invest in the open source tools they depend on and helping ensure your integration stays maintained and up to date.

[**See how sponsorship works →**](../how-to-sponsor/)

### For Individuals

If Apprise has saved you time or solved a problem, even a small contribution helps cover API costs and keeps the project active.
